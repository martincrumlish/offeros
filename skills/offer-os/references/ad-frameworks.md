# Ad Frameworks

Facebook creative is targeting. Ads should attract buyers, amplify intent, and filter low-intent browsers.

## Awareness Selection

### Unaware

Use pattern interrupt, curiosity with intent, identity callout, myth-busting, and problem discovery.

Avoid heavy offer details and price-led ads.

### Problem-Aware

Use pain, consequence, diagnostic, and desired outcome.

Best frameworks:

- PAS
- Before-After-Bridge
- Cost of Inaction
- Mistake-Based
- Checklist/Diagnostic

### Solution-Aware

Use mechanism, proof, comparison, and differentiation.

Best frameworks:

- Problem-Mechanism-Proof
- 4P
- Demonstration
- Comparison
- Case Study

### Product-Aware

Use offer stack, urgency, bonuses, guarantee, and retargeting proof.

Best frameworks:

- Offer-CTA
- Testimonial-Proof-CTA
- Objection-Reversal
- Deadline/Bonus
- FAQ Ad

## Core Structures

- PAS: clear pain and direct solution
- Before-After-Bridge: vivid transformation
- 4P: promise, picture, proof, push
- Problem-Mechanism-Proof: skeptical markets
- Myth-Busting: false market beliefs
- Mistake-Based: diagnostics, audits, lead magnets
- Case Study: strong proof
- Demonstration: visual products, tools, dashboards, templates
- Offer-CTA: warm retargeting
- Objection-Reversal: known hesitation

## Creative Concept Rules

Choose the image based on what must be believed:

- result: proof, before/after, testimonial, metric
- mechanism: diagram, framework, process visual
- attention: pattern interrupt, bold statement, contrast
- trust: founder, workshop, behind-the-scenes, authority
- product: mockup, dashboard, template, deliverable screenshot

## Required Ad Output

For robust campaigns, create:

- market diagnosis
- 15 angles
- 30 hooks
- 10 creative concepts
- 10 complete ads
- best 3 ads to test first
- 3 finished ad images from imagegen when image generation is available, with provenance recorded in the manifest

Do not count plain text cards, HTML/CSS cards, screenshots, or generated-by-code PNGs as generated ad images. Do not create ads with Pillow/PIL shapes at all. In deep generated-design runs non-imagegen assets cannot satisfy the required 3 ad creatives; if imagegen is blocked, mark the ad images `needs_revision`, record the blocker, and keep the run out of complete status. Do not create SVG ad images.
