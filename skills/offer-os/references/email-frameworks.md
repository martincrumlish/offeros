# Email Frameworks

Choose one sequence-level framework, then give each email one job.

## Product Launch Formula

Use for launches with open-cart and close-cart windows.

Flow:

- opportunity
- transformation/teaching
- proof/objections
- open cart
- objection handling
- urgency
- final deadline

Use only with a real launch window, expiring bonus, cohort start, or price change.

## Soap Opera Sequence

Use for personal brands, coaching, consulting, and trust-building.

Flow:

- set the stage
- conflict/problem
- discovery/turning point
- new mechanism
- invitation/offer

## Problem-Agitation-Solution Sequence

Use for short evergreen sales campaigns and direct promotions.

Flow:

- problem recognition
- cost of inaction
- solution
- proof
- CTA

## Belief-Shift Sequence

Use for sophisticated buyers, new mechanisms, and offers blocked by false beliefs.

Flow:

- false belief
- cost of belief
- new insight
- proof
- offer
- objection reversal

## Individual Email Jobs

- Story-Lesson-Offer for nurture
- 4P for offer announcements
- PAS for urgent sales pushes
- Objection Handling for a single conversion blocker
- Proof Email when trust is the blocker
- Deadline Email only when urgency is real

## Output Standard

For each email include:

- subject lines
- preview text
- body copy
- CTA
- send timing
- campaign role

Format in clean HTML when the user wants easy copying into an email app.

Do not paste the same product-description paragraph across the sequence. Each email must have a distinct job and distinct body copy. Repeated body blocks, missing send timing, missing preview text, or missing campaign role fail deep mode.
