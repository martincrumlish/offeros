# Product Reveal Framework

Product reveal is not a short "introducing" paragraph. It is the bridge between belief change and the offer stack.

Required `productReveal` fields:

- `productType`
- `plainEnglishDescription`
- `whoItIsFor`
- `whatItHelpsThemDo`
- `whyNow`
- `coreComponents`
- `howItWorksSteps`
- `lookInsideProof`
- `differenceFromAlternatives`
- `bridgeToOfferStack`

Each `coreComponents` row must include:

- `feature`
- `benefit`
- `reasonItMatters`
- `buyerProblemSolved`
- `proofOrPreview`
- `plainBullet`

Good component bullet pattern:

`[Feature] so [specific buyer benefit], which matters because [real consequence or decision it fixes].`

Do not ship a product reveal that only says "PDF, workbook, templates, videos, bonuses" without the benefit and reason each piece matters.
